# Responsible Use

Talibus is a research and systems engineering prototype for
imperfect-information game AI. It is not intended for gambling automation or
live poker decision support.

## Not Intended Uses

- Not for real-money poker play.
- Not for live decision support during online or in-person poker.
- Not for overlays, real-time assistance, or RTA workflows.
- Not for casino or poker-site automation.
- Not for bypassing platform rules, account restrictions, or fair-play systems.
- Not for claiming solved poker, real-world profitability, human-level
  strength, solver-level strength, or proven multiplayer Deep CFR convergence.

## Intended Uses

- Research and education around imperfect-information games.
- Simulation and evaluation harness design.
- AI systems engineering practice.
- Rust/Python/ONNX integration experiments.
- Portfolio review of a research prototype.
- Reproducibility and documentation exercises around controlled simulator
  results.

## Result Framing

Published results in this repository should be interpreted as controlled
simulator measurements against scripted baseline opponents. They are useful for
comparing runs inside the codebase and for documenting research progress. They
are not evidence of real-money performance or general poker strength.
